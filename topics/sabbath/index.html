Sabbath Bible answers

Read clear Bible answers related to sabbath, with Scripture references for each question.

  • Did the Apostles Keep the Sabbath?

    Yes. Acts repeatedly records the apostles teaching and worshiping on the Sabbath.

  • Was the Sabbath Changed to Sunday?

    The Bible records events occurring on the first day of the week, but it does not record a command changing the seventh-day Sabbath to Sunday.

  • Why Do Christians Worship on Sunday?

    Many Christians worship on Sunday because of the association of Sunday with Christ's resurrection and because Sunday worship became established through Christian history and tradition. Scripture itself records some first-day gatherings, but does not command Sunday as the replacement for the seventh-day Sabbath.

  • Does the Bible Command Sunday Worship?

    No explicit biblical command requires Christians to keep Sunday as a holy day.

  • What Does Romans 14:5 Mean?

    Romans 14:5 discusses differences among believers concerning days and must be understood within its immediate context of disputes about eating, drinking, and personal religious observance. The verse does not explicitly mention the weekly Sabbath.

  • What Does "Lord's Day" Mean in the Bible?

    Revelation 1:10 says John was "in the Spirit" on "the Lord's day," but the verse itself does not explicitly identify which day of the week John meant.

  • Is the Sabbath still important for Christians?

    The Sabbath points back to creation, honors God's commandments, and offers a weekly rhythm of worship, rest, mercy, and delight in the Lord. Jesus kept the Sabbath and taught that it was made as a blessing for humanity.

  • What role do God's commandments have in the Christian life?

    The Bible teaches that we are saved by grace through faith, not by earning God's favor. At the same time, God's commandments reveal His character and guide the grateful obedience of those who love Him.

  • What does the Bible teach about creation?

    The Bible presents God as the intentional Creator of the heavens, the earth, and humanity in His own image. Creation reveals God's power and care, and it is the foundation for the Sabbath rest.

  • What is the message of the first angel in Revelation 14?

    The first angel calls the world to fear God, give Him glory, and worship the Creator, announcing that the hour of judgment has arrived.

  • Did God really create the world in six literal days?

    Scripture describes creation as six literal days followed by a seventh day of rest, a pattern later memorialized in the weekly Sabbath commandment.
