Second Coming Bible answers

Read clear Bible answers related to second coming, with Scripture references for each question.

  • Do You Go to Heaven When You Die?

    Scripture's emphasis is not that the righteous immediately go to heaven at death. The Bible repeatedly points believers toward the resurrection at Christ's second coming as the time when the dead in Christ receive immortal life.

  • What Is the Rapture?

    The Bible does not use the word "rapture," but it does teach that Christ's followers will be caught up to meet Him in the air when He returns.

  • How can I be ready for the Second Coming of Jesus?

    Readiness for Jesus' return is not date-setting or fear. It is an active relationship with Christ that shows itself in faith, watchfulness, obedience, service, and hope.

  • What was the Day of Atonement and why does it matter today?

    The Day of Atonement pictured a final cleansing of sin's record, pointing forward to the judgment and to Christ's ongoing work of removing sin completely.

  • What are the biblical signs that Jesus is coming soon?

    Jesus described spiritual, social, and global signs — false christs, wars, disasters, and the gospel reaching the whole world — as markers that His return is near.
