Why Do Christians Worship on Sunday?

Many Christians worship on Sunday because of the association of Sunday with Christ's resurrection and because Sunday worship became established through Christian history and tradition. Scripture itself records some first-day gatherings, but does not command Sunday as the replacement for the seventh-day Sabbath.

What Does Romans 14:5 Mean?

Colossians 2:16 warns Christians not to allow others to judge them concerning food, drink, festivals, new moons, or sabbaths. The passage is understood by many Bible readers as referring to the ceremonial calendar and its associated sabbaths because these things were shadows pointing to Christ.
