Creation

Does God Exist?

Yes. The Bible presents the existence of God as the foundation of everything else it teaches. It does not attempt to prove God's existence through philosophical argument. Instead, it begins with the declaration, “In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth” (Genesis 1:1). Scripture teaches that God's existence can also be recognized through His creation.

Who Is God?

The Bible teaches that God is the eternal Creator, the source of life, the sovereign ruler of the universe, and the embodiment of perfect love, holiness, righteousness, wisdom, and truth. God is not merely a force or abstract power. Scripture presents Him as personal, intelligent, loving, and capable of communicating with His creatures.
