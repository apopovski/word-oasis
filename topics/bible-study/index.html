Is the Bible True?

Yes. The Bible presents itself as the inspired Word of God and repeatedly describes God's Word as true, trustworthy, and enduring. The Bible says: “All scripture is given by inspiration of God” (2 Timothy 3:16). And Jesus prayed: “Thy word is truth” (John 17:17). The question of whether the Bible is true ultimately involves whether God can be trusted.
