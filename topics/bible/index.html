Is the Bible Reliable and Trustworthy?

The Bible presents itself as God's trustworthy revelation, points readers to Jesus, and invites careful examination rather than blind acceptance. Its message should be tested by Scripture itself and understood in context.

What Does the Bible Say About Dreams?

The Bible records that God sometimes used dreams to reveal His purposes, but it also warns against trusting every claimed message. Dreams should be tested by Scripture and never replace God's written Word.

How Do I Study the Bible?

Study the Bible prayerfully, in context, by comparing related passages and asking what the text meant before applying it to life. The goal is not merely information but a clearer view of God and a faithful response to His Word.

What Happens in the Investigative Judgment?

The Bible teaches that God judges with complete knowledge, justice, and mercy before Christ returns. The judgment reveals God's righteous character, considers every person's response to His grace, and points believers to Christ as their Advocate and High Priest.

What Is Christian Meditation?

Biblical meditation means prayerfully dwelling on God's character, works, and Word so that truth shapes the mind and life. It is not emptying the mind or seeking spiritual guidance apart from God and Scripture.
